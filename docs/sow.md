# Project Overview

HSE Pacific is fitting a camera and auto-stop system to Liebherr 922 excavators for Rio Tinto. The system is intended to detect a person in the machine's path and stop the machine, using stopping systems already on the machine.

Rio Tinto has asked for an engineering report on the installation before a wider rollout. HSE Pacific has nominated Comptune Engineering report B2600003A as the form of that report. B2600003A records an inspection of a Komatsu WA470-8 wheel loader at Veolia Chullora on 1 July 2026.

Switch will review the drawings and paperwork already given to Liebherr, inspect one sample excavator, and write the report from that inspection. The report identifies the machine, describes what is fitted and how it joins the existing stopping systems, names the standard reviewed, and states whether the method is suitable and on what conditions.

The machine is still to be confirmed. A machine in Perth is preferred. A machine in Karratha is acceptable if a machine in Perth cannot be found.

# In Scope

**Document review**

- Review of the drawings and paperwork HSE Pacific has supplied to Liebherr for this installation
- Review of the machine functionality videos HSE Pacific provides

**Site inspection**

- Inspection of one sample Liebherr 922 excavator, in Perth or, if none is available there, in Karratha
- Record of the machine identity, what is fitted, and where the cameras and associated equipment are mounted
- Record of how the installation joins the stopping systems already on the machine, and whether the operator can still command a stop beyond the installation
- A job hazard analysis completed on site before the inspection starts

**Engineering report**

- A report in the form of Comptune Engineering report B2600003A, written from the document review and the site inspection
- Cover identification of the client, the modification, the sample machine (make, model, serial, and PIN), the document reference, the inspection date and location, and the standard reviewed
- Background on what the installation is intended to do
- Inspection findings, the verification required after fitment, and a short conclusion on whether the method is suitable and the conditions on fitment and verification
- Signature of the inspecting Switch engineer, with professional registration and the date
- Appendices for the machine specification and the installation wiring, from material HSE Pacific supplies

**Site mobilisation**

- Names and dates of availability of the Switch people attending, and confirmation that required qualifications are current
- Switch certificate of currency for the insurances the site requires
- A test plan describing the equipment, the tasks, and the time on site

# Out of Scope

- Design, supply, or installation of the camera or auto-stop system
- Modification of the sample machine or of its OEM systems
- An endorsement by Liebherr, or a regulatory type approval
- Inspection of more than one machine, a fleet rollout, production install procedures, or training
- Rio Tinto's acceptance of the report, and onboarding of Switch as a Rio Tinto supplier beyond the particulars for this visit
- HSE Pacific product development, training, or other work outside this installation review
